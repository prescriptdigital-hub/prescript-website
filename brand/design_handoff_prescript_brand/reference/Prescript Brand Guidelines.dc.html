01 — The idea

A prescription, written in code.

A doctor doesn’t hand you every medicine on the shelf. They diagnose, then write one precise prescription. Prescript does the same for businesses: we look at what holds a business back, and prescribe the exact brand, platform, marketing, automation or AI it needs. Nothing more, nothing less.

The mark draws that idea as a line of code, < · >, turned 45° into a diamond.

<

The opening bracket: diagnose

We start by framing the real problem: the bottleneck, the lost lead, the brand that doesn’t earn trust. Every solution opens with understanding.

>

The closing bracket: deliver

We close the loop by building and shipping. The two strokes interlock but never touch: our expertise and the client’s business, working as one while each keeps its own shape.

The square: the prescription

The one precise answer at the centre of everything. It comes back as the full stop after the wordmark: decided, delivered, done. Execution is the product.

◇

The diamond: lasting value

Together the brackets form a diamond, the shape of something valuable that holds under pressure. It is left slightly open, because a good prescription keeps improving.

Imagery

Real people doing real work, in natural light. Show the craft: hands, whiteboards, screens, the moment a problem gets solved. Images are either shown as they are, or as a brick/oxblood duotone for brand moments.

AVOID Stock handshakes, globes, circuit boards, light bulbs, glowing robot heads, neon gradients, and text placed directly on busy photos.
